# Performance report

Historical device/scale measurements remain in versioned v1.0.1 evidence. They do not certify new v0.4 sensor computations, White Cell reconstruction or Crew workloads. The new logical clock is callback-independent at 30 Hz, catches up at most five ticks after stalls, discards excess wall-time backlog and suspends when the document is hidden. These limits are surfaced to the user; continuous background operation is not promised.

White Cell cache is bounded, input-addressed and returns copies. Audit claim that every ordinary dashboard poll reconstructs all completed sessions was disproven; legacy stats backfill and multiple SQL/profile reads are separate performance concerns.

Fresh v0.4 contact-scale, real render FPS, poll/cache/HTTP latency, concurrent clients and memory measurements are pending. Rendering may lower detail or use Tactical 2D without reducing simulation/scoring fidelity.
