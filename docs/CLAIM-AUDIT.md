## Official submission format

The official [2026 template](https://sih.gov.in/letters/2026/SIH2026-IDEA-Presentation-Format.pptx) was downloaded and verified byte-identical to the supplied template. The main PDF has six slides including title and retains TITLE PAGE, IDEA TITLE / Proposed Solution, TECHNICAL APPROACH, FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY, IMPACT AND BENEFITS, RESEARCH AND REFERENCES; its instruction slide is omitted. No college/member names are required in these official title fields and the user explicitly requested their omission.

The live [official PS page](https://sih.gov.in/sih2026PS), checked 3 October 2026, lists SIH26247, Ministry of Defence / Defence Services Staff College, Software, Robotics and Drones. Its displayed deadline is 5 October; the team's target stays 4 October. The [public guidelines](https://sih.gov.in/letters/2026/SIH%202026%20Guidelines.pdf) retain an older 30 September date, so authenticated portal/SPOC deadlines should govern. Public guidelines require a presentation PDF; no current public video duration/size limit was found. The prepared videos follow the requested 2–3-minute / ~2-minute targets. Portal submission itself is not performed by this package.

## Final limitations

Dedicated physical WAN-off/LAN-on validation was not completed before submission. Multi-device LAN operation, local asset serving, process-outage recovery and queued-event resynchronization were validated separately.
